About LinguaViva

LinguaViva is an advanced AI translation platform dedicated to the preservation and revitalization of low-resource languages in Mindanao. Powered by a fine-tuned NLLB-200 neural network, our system accurately translates major regional languages like Cebuano and Filipino into endangered indigenous dialects such as Kagan (Kalagan) and Manobo.

By leveraging state-of-the-art Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) techniques, LinguaViva breaks language barriers and ensures that cultural heritage is empowered for future generations.

System Disclaimer and Limitations

In compliance with academic research standards and ethical governance regarding indigenous data, please note that LinguaViva is strictly an experimental system. Because the platform handles sensitive domains such as health and education, users are advised that automated outputs should not be treated as absolute or authoritative. The system is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or administrative advice. The developers and affiliated institutions assume no liability for the misinterpretation or direct application of generated translations in critical real-world scenarios.
